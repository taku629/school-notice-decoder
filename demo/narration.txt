School notices can hide the next step in a wall of unfamiliar words.
School Notice Decoder turns a photo into a plan for multilingual families.
This is a fictional school notice. Tesseract reads the image in the browser, and the parent can correct the text.
Before optional AI analysis, the student’s name is masked locally. The exact outgoing text remains visible for review.
The no-key demo uses a clearly labeled sample engine, not a live AI response. Here, Spanish instructions become checkable actions. Every task links back to its source.
Calendar dates stay editable. Only after a parent checks them can an all-day reminder file be downloaded. No timezone is guessed.
Gemini is optional. The prototype allows synthetic, non-sensitive content only, and explains its free-tier data use.
The goal is not another translation. It is a next step a family can inspect, understand, and act on.
