HONEST BY DESIGN

Translation is a beginning.
Understanding is the goal.

Every action links back to the words it came from. Names can be masked before optional AI analysis, dates can be corrected, and nothing goes into your calendar until you choose to export it.

What leaves your browser?

Local engines: no notice text is sent to a cloud AI. OCR and optional English → Spanish translation run in the browser. Scripts and model files download from CDNs and Hugging Face; this is not fully offline.

Optional Gemini: only the masked text you review is sent to Google with your key. Free-tier data-use policies apply. Use synthetic, non-sensitive notices only.

Storage: no app database, analytics, cookies or notice history. Text and keys are not saved by the app; model files may be browser-cached. Check output with your school before relying on it.
